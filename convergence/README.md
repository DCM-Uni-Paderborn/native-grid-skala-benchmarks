# Numerical Checks in the PCCP ESI

Only the reported ACONF cutoff series, self-consistent GPW water quadrature,
45 periodic-water force/stress executions, and seven full/reduced k-point
comparisons are included. The isolated-cell padding checks are under
`diagnostics/molecular-padding-convergence`; molecular-crystal controls are
under `benchmarks/X23-mini/results/controls`.

These bounded checks are not a claim of universal convergence.
The exact scope and limitations are defined in the ESI.
