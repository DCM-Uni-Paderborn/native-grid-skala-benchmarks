# Reported Basis Sensitivity

`index.json` freezes the reported 17 September 2026 assessment and links its
87 compact execution records to their exact inputs and outputs:
eight CO2/NH3 controls, 30 paired molecular controls, three ethane state/grid
controls, 28 ice/symmetry controls, and 18 solid basis/runtime controls.
The four earlier Urea controls remain in the X23-mini dataset and are not
duplicated here.

`assessment.json` is regenerated by `scripts/basis_sensitivity.py`. It separates
paired basis sensitivity from benchmark accuracy, preserves the initial
high-energy QZ ethane solution alongside the lower-state restart, and
restricts the ice comparison to the same twelve phases. Relative ice energies
cancel the molecular reference. The Si, diamond and MgO fits use matched
five-volume subsets and do not establish ten-solid QZ basis convergence.
The existing 70-reaction statistics remain unchanged. All raw results needed
for the reported comparisons are preserved, without executable binaries,
model weights, WFN files, unfinished campaigns, or unreported structures.
