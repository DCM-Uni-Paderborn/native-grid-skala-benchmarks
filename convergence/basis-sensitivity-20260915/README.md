# Reported Basis Sensitivity

`index.json` freezes the reported 15 September 2026 assessment and links its
48 additional compact execution records to their exact inputs and outputs:
eight CO2/NH3 controls, 24 molecular controls, and 16 ice/symmetry controls.
The four earlier Urea controls remain in the X23-mini dataset and are not
duplicated here.

`assessment.json` is regenerated by `scripts/basis_sensitivity.py`. It separates
paired basis sensitivity from benchmark accuracy, identifies the unresolved
QZ ethane outlier, and restricts the ice comparison to the same six phases.
The existing 70-reaction statistics remain unchanged. All raw results needed
for the reported comparisons are preserved, without executable binaries,
model weights, WFN files, unfinished campaigns, or unreported structures.
