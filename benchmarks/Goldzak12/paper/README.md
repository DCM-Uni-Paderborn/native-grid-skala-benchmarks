# Manuscript and SI assets

These assets correspond to the selected ten-solid structural comparison in
the Native-grid Skala manuscript, Overleaf project
https://www.overleaf.com/project/6a860f5e29d899a1050644a1.

- `periodic-summary.tex`: main-text common-ten error table.
- `periodic-tables-si.tex`: native values, fit diagnostics, matched-source
  statistics, and actual volume-resolved k meshes.
- `periodic-analysis-si.tex`: SI discussion and qualifications.
- `../results/eos-literature-residuals.png`: signed-error figure.

Tables are regenerated by `../scripts/build_paper_tables.py` from selected
CSV data, not copied from rounded manuscript values. The manuscript supplies
the REVTeX preamble, method macros and bibliography. Source identifiers and
DOI conventions are in `../reference/SOURCES.md`.

The findings describe the retained data and their limitations. They do not
claim a complete final-setting error budget, universal symmetry validation,
or cohesive energies. No failed calculation is promoted by inclusion here.
