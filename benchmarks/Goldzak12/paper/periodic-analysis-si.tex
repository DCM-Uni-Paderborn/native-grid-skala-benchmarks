\section{Selected Periodic Equations of State}

The structural analysis uses AlN, AlP, BN, BP, C, LiCl, MgO, MgS, Si, and SiC from the Goldzak12 set.\cite{Goldzak2022Solids} AlN and AlP use volume indices v01--v09 in every representation, and the other eight solids use v01--v10. The original volume ratios are evenly spaced between 0.90 and 1.10 relative to the static-lattice experimental cell. Thus the nine-point windows end at a ratio of 1.0777778. All cells are conventional cubic eight-atom cells, and all fits use the same third-order Birch--Murnaghan form. The 40 method--solid entries derive from 36 independent curves and 352 unique energies; the AE curves for BN and C are reused for both mixed-core protocols. No cohesive energies are reported.

LiF and LiH are excluded from every method's structural statistics. The LiF \texttt{GAPW\_XC}--\GTH{} curve was inconsistent despite formal SCF convergence, and the AE LiF minimum was outside the sampled window. For LiH, the AE and \texttt{GAPW\_XC}--\GTH{} minima lay outside that window. The cause of the LiF inconsistency remains unresolved; it is not attributed solely to failure of the SCF solver. The original data are preserved. The exclusions and common endpoint selection are based on EOS availability and quality, not on reducing errors against experiment.

\subsection{Executed settings and numerical scope}

The archived executed inputs of all 352 selected points use a plane-wave cutoff of 800~Ry, relative cutoff of 60~Ry, 150 radial points, the 770-point Lebedev rule, and the \texttt{EXT\_SMALL} one-center basis. The UZH 2026.2 AE/GTH basis and potential definitions and native atom-composite construction are retained. Each calculation is charge neutral and spin restricted. The exact input files document dispersion, integral thresholds, mixing or OT settings, and software/model provenance. Traditional diagonalization uses \texttt{EPS\_SCF}$=5\times10^{-6}$; OT uses $5\times10^{-7}$. SCF histories and restart choices can differ between accepted executions, but neither thresholds nor grids are loosened for the reported analysis.

Table~\ref{tab:actual-k-meshes} gives the actual Gamma-centered meshes. The same mesh is used by all representations at a given volume. A volume-dependent mesh can change within a curve, however, and a smooth EOS does not by itself rule out the resulting sampling error. Selected symmetry controls establish equivalence only for the tested geometry, mesh, and executable; they are not a general proof for every point-group reduction or production restart.

Execution acceptance requires the relevant newest program block to converge and terminate normally without a later abort, zero execution/timing exit status, and consistent input hashes and physical electron counts. The atom-grid electron integral is retained as a separate quadrature diagnostic, not as an additional binary SCF-validity criterion. These checks do not establish a complete numerical uncertainty budget. The earlier cutoff, atom-grid, and k-point controls require protocol-matched assessment before assigning universal errors to the final periodic settings. In particular, neither a 0.002~\AA{} lattice-error bound nor a 1~GPa bulk-modulus bound is claimed here.

Every selected full-window minimum is bracketed, and the largest fit RMS residual is 0.674~meV per atom. Endpoint-omission tests use the remaining points without recomputing electronic energies. The maximum lattice shift is 0.002444~\AA{} for HD MgS when v01 is omitted. The maximum bulk-modulus shift is 9.997~GPa for GX AlN when v09 is omitted from its nine-point window. Removing the smallest-volume point can destroy minimum bracketing for LiCl; that point is retained. Endpoint sensitivity is distinct from discretization error and is particularly relevant to curvature-derived bulk moduli. The full per-curve tests are supplied in the benchmark repository.

\subsection{Representation comparison}

GX denotes all-GTH \texttt{GAPW\_XC}; AE denotes all-electron \GAPW{}; HD and HOC denote mixed \GAPW{}-AE/\GTH{} with direct and one-center GTH fields, respectively. ``Hybrid'' here refers to the mixed core treatment, not to a hybrid XC functional such as HSE06. The mixed routes retain AE H, Li, B, C, N, O, and F and use GTH Mg, Al, Si, P, S, and Cl. All-electron GAPW and GX intrinsically include their respective one-center density terms; the independent with/without comparison is HD versus HOC.

All selected native lattice constants are below the primary experimental reference. The GX--AE mean absolute differences are 0.01893~\AA{} and 10.55~GPa for $a_0$ and $B_0$. This comparison changes the core Hamiltonian and basis and cannot isolate quadrature accuracy. At fixed mixed-core Hamiltonian, HOC--HD changes $a_0$ by 0.000135~\AA{} on average and at most 0.000542~\AA{} (AlP). The corresponding bulk-modulus changes average 1.11~GPa and reach 5.75~GPa (MgO). Restricting that average to the eight independent mixed-core pairs gives 0.000169~\AA{} and 1.39~GPa, respectively. The correction therefore leaves the main contraction trend intact but can affect EOS curvature appreciably.

\subsection{Matched literature comparisons}

All signed errors are calculated minus the same static-lattice experimental values transcribed from Goldzak et al.,\cite{Goldzak2022Solids} not method-specific experimental adjustments. Tables below give native values, fit diagnostics, and matched literature errors. System-resolved source values and signed residuals accompany the SI in machine-readable form. Each literature comparison uses its exact intersection with the selected native set; statistics with different sample sizes are not combined into a ranking.

HF, MP2, SCS-MP2, and SOS-MP2 are compared on all ten selected solids using Tables II and III of Goldzak et al. Zhang et al. provide LDA, PBE, PBEsol, M06-L, SCAN, and HSE06 values for nine of these solids (AlN is absent).\cite{Zhang2018Solids} Their SI Tables III and V distinguish static electronic values (``Uncorr.'') from predictions with zero-point contributions (``Corr.''); only the former enter the comparison with the static-lattice reference. Their SCAN energies were evaluated non-self-consistently on PBE orbitals/densities. These numerical conventions are not identical to the present native calculations.

Schimka et al. supply PBE, PBEsol, HSE06, and HSEsol lattice constants for nine selected solids, excluding MgS.\cite{Schimka2011HSEsol} Bulk moduli were not extracted from their separate supplement and are not inferred. The self-consistent Tao--Mo results of Mo et al. cover eight selected solids, excluding AlN and BN.\cite{Mo2017Solids} Their lattice constants and bulk moduli are rescored against the Goldzak reference, which differs in places from the experimental reference used in the original paper. GFN1-xTB and GFN2-xTB lattice constants from Table S3 of our periodic GFN manuscript\cite{Alizadeh2026PeriodicGFN2} are compared on nine shared solids: its LC10 excludes MgO and LiH, whereas the present set excludes LiF and LiH. That curated table does not supply bulk moduli.

On the matched ten-solid set, SCS-MP2 and SOS-MP2 lattice MAEs are 0.0125 and 0.0149~\AA{}, versus 0.0728--0.0844~\AA{} for the native protocols. The corresponding bulk-modulus MAEs are 4.10 and 3.88~GPa, versus 16.61--21.77~GPa. Established semilocal and screened-hybrid methods also have smaller lattice errors on their matched subsets. This comparison demonstrates a limitation of the present structural predictions; it does not identify whether the dominant contribution is functional or numerical.

Goldzak et al. also list alternate experimental bulk moduli of 410.2, 168.0, and 169.8~GPa for BN, BP, and MgO, respectively, instead of the primary 388.5, 176.5, and 173.0~GPa. Using all three alternate values simultaneously lowers each native ten-solid bulk-modulus MAE by 1.00~GPa. This reference sensitivity does not remove the main discrepancy and is not a numerical-convergence correction.

The PBE/PBEsol/SCAN aggregate errors 0.061/0.030/0.030~\AA{} and 12.2/7.8/7.4~GPa listed in the broader literature coincide with the 44-solid statistics of Tran et al.\cite{Tran2016Solids} They are not used as ten- or twelve-solid matched errors. The system-resolved Zhang data instead provide the matched DFT comparison. This distinction avoids attributing differences in sample composition to the native implementation.
